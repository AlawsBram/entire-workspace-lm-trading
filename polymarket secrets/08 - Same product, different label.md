# 08 · Same Product, Different Label — EU binary option vs Polymarket 5-min bet

> Source: [dyutam.com](https://dyutam.com/news/polymarket-5-minute-bitcoin-bets-60m-bots-retail/#same-product-different-label) · captured 2026-09-25

| dimension | EU binary option (banned for retail since 2018) | Polymarket 5-min BTC bet (CFTC-regulated event contract) |
|---|---|---|
| Product structure | Fixed payout or zero based on whether underlying meets predetermined condition | Fixed payout ($1) or zero based on whether BTC is above/below threshold |
| Duration | Seconds to minutes (typically) | 5 minutes |
| Underlying asset | Forex pairs, stock indices, crypto, commodities | Bitcoin (also ETH, SOL, XRP) |
| Resolution mechanism | Provider's proprietary price feed | Chainlink decentralized oracle |
| Retail account loss rate | 74–89% of accounts lost money (ESMA data) | Unknown — no disclosure required |
| Average retail losses | €1,600 to €29,000 per client (ESMA data) | Unknown — no disclosure required |
| Why banned / why not | "Structural expected negative return," "embedded conflict of interest between providers and clients," "complexity and lack of transparency" | Not evaluated under these criteria |
| Consumer protections | Product banned entirely for retail | None required |
| Deposit limits | N/A — product prohibited | None |
| Loss limits | N/A — product prohibited | None |
| Self-exclusion database | N/A — product prohibited | None |
| Responsible gambling features | N/A — product prohibited | None |
| Regulator | ESMA / national competent authorities (FCA, AMF, BaFin, etc.) | CFTC (U.S. derivatives regulator) |
| Legal classification | Banned financial instrument | Regulated event contract |

ESMA banned binary options across the EU in July 2018 after finding that the majority of retail accounts lost money. The products described above are structurally identical. The difference is jurisdictional classification, not product design.

Source cited in widget: ESMA Decision (EU) 2018/795
