# I Tried Two More Ways to Beat Polymarket (Part 4)

Source: https://research.marvoproduction-apps.de/two-more-ways-to-beat-polymarket/
Captured 2026-10-07, verbatim (footer cards trimmed).

Follow-up after the series: readers' two best "but did you try X" ideas.
Still paper, no real money, not advice. Ending first: still efficient — but
one idea got further into his head than anything in the original ten.

## The first idea: let a sharper market do my thinking
Use a sharp sportsbook's (Pinnacle's) closing line as a signal; when bookie
and prediction market disagree, trust the bookie.
Died on a fact, not a calculation: Polymarket does not sell the bet needed.
Every single-match market resolving in the next 10 days: 331 markets; only 3
were "will this team win"; 328 were over/under, BTTS, halftime-draw confetti.
The World Cup sells only as "who lifts the trophy", never "who wins
Tuesday's game". "You cannot run a horse race when the track only sells hot
dogs." Same shape as his earlier calendar-arb failure: two things that
looked like the same bet and were not. Sometimes market structure ends the
idea before the math is invited.

## The second idea: stop betting, become the house  ← read this one twice
Market-making: quote both sides, keep the spread, collect rent. Rebuilt what
a patient maker would have earned from the public trade tape: filled at the
prices takers actually paid, carried to resolution.
**First number: +4.6%** per dollar provided, CI above zero. "For one warm
afternoon I was the house."
Then he tried to kill it. Two cuts:
1. **Toxicity** — weight toward the LARGE trades, the poisoned fills a maker
   at the back of the queue actually catches (someone informed running you
   over as the price moves).
2. **More markets** — the 4.6 came from a small handful, and small handfuls
   lie.
**It flipped: −5.5%, whole CI below zero. On the biggest, most informed
trades: worse, past −7%. Early markets negative, late markets negative. And
the 30-minute markout had been sitting at zero the entire time.**
"The spread you capture is exactly the spread the smart money takes back
off you." The +4.6 was the 16-wallet ghost again: a small sample wearing a
genius's coat.

## Two more doors, same lock
Caveat: these two were fast probes, not the full pre-registered treatment —
they didn't need it. One died on a fact readable off the order book in an
afternoon; the other on sample size, "the most ordinary cause of death there
is for a trading idea, and the one almost nobody writes down".
Twelve angles, same answer: the price is sharper than the sportsbook he
tried to borrow, sharper than the spread he tried to farm, sharper than him
on his best day.
