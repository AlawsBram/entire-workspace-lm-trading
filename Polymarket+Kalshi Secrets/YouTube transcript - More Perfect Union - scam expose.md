## Key claims & numbers (for research cross-reference)

- **Profit concentration:** one analysis — 0.04% of traders captured ~70% of
  profits. (Consistent direction with Gómez-Cram's ~3% price-discovery
  minority and overround.pro's "measurable minority" — the P&L distribution
  is extreme-skewed.)
- **Accuracy (Vanderbilt study, 2024 election):** markets better than chance:
  PredictIt 93%, Kalshi 78%, Polymarket 67%. NOTE: different metric than
  marv's calibration test (0.982 price vs 0.9765 actual) — "better than
  chance on outcomes" ≠ "miscalibrated prices"; not a contradiction.
- **Jobs-report miss:** markets forecast +60k Feb jobs; report came in down,
  ~150k delta.
- **Usage mix:** Kalshi 85–90% sports; Polymarket ~40% sports / 60% other.
- **Losses:** Citizens Bank analyst report — prediction-market traders lost
  MORE than they would have at traditional sportsbooks in the first 3 months.
- **"No house" caveat:** Kalshi is sometimes the counterparty on event
  contracts (collects full winnings when you lose) — a house does exist in
  some products.
- **Regulation:** Kalshi sued by over a dozen states (class actions claim
  deceived sports bettors); CFTC asserts authority; state sportsbook tax
  ~51% (NY) is what federal CFTC regulation avoids; age of participation 18
  vs 21 at sportsbooks; Kalshi U campus program shut down; AOC calls for
  tobacco-style ad bans; Sen. Chris Murphy: markets where outcomes are known
  to insiders "shouldn't be traded at all"; Robin Hanson (cited by Polymarket
  CEO): insiders are "great" for accuracy.
- **Insider framing:** GTA VI / Drake / OpenAI release-date markets; the
  Super Bowl anthem-length listener with a directional mic; oil traders
  ahead of an Iran announcement — insider policing is argued to be
  unscalable.
- **Marketing to economic anxiety:** free grocery store stunt, "you'll make
  rent" ads, Kalshi $1B March Madness bracket prize, "90 minutes left to
  attempt generational wealth" (CEO), influencer networks incl. targeted
  outreach to young women.

   State lawsuits, CFTC posture, possible ad bans or market-category

   regime_watch's legal/rulebook sources + the changelog mirrors are our

   reason our stack is maker/measurement-side only. We are explicitly trying
   to join the tiny skilled minority via instrumentation, not via hero bets.

   price calibration (honest to ~0.5%) vs Vanderbilt's outcome-beats-chance
   (67%). Critics conflate them; we won't.
